Our Story

Named after the world's most precious spice

Saffron was born from a simple idea: bring the bold flavours of Lahore, the royal richness of Delhi and the fragrant freshness of Bangkok together under one roof. Every dish is prepared from scratch by chefs trained in their own regional traditions.

Whether it's a family dinner, a date night or a celebration with friends, we set the table so you can taste your way across Asia without leaving Toronto.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Is all your meat Halal?

Yes — all meat and poultry served at Saffron is 100% Halal certified.

How far in advance can I book a table?

You can pre-book up to 60 days ahead. Same-day bookings are welcome subject to availability.

Do you host private events?

Yes, we host birthdays, corporate dinners and festive gatherings for up to 60 guests with custom set menus. Call us to plan your event.

Can I adjust the spice level?

Absolutely. Every dish can be prepared mild, medium or hot — just let your server know.

Do you offer takeout and delivery?

Yes, call us for takeout or order through popular delivery apps across Toronto.
