From the Kitchen

Fireside &
Hand-ground

Every masala is toasted and ground in-house each morning. Our breads come from a clay tandoor, our curries from recipes carried from Lahore, Delhi and Bangkok.

Nothing is rushed. Nihari braises overnight, dal simmers for twelve hours, and every plate leaves the pass finished by hand.

— The Saffron Kitchen
Three Kitchens

One table, three traditions.

Order across the menu and share. A karahi, a curry and a bowl of noodles side by side is how Saffron is meant to be eaten.

01 — Pakistani

Flavours of Lahore

Smoky karahis, slow nihari, seekh kebabs and fragrant biryani from the homes and streets of Pakistan.

02 — Indian

The Royal Table

Butter chicken, dal makhani, rogan josh and tandoori breads baked fresh in our clay oven.

03 — Thai

Bangkok Street

Wok-tossed pad thai, coconut curries, tom yum and bright salads with fresh herbs.

Good to Know
Is all your meat Halal?

Yes — all meat and poultry at Saffron is 100% Halal certified.

How far ahead can I book a table?

Up to 60 days in advance. Same-day bookings are welcome when tables are available.

Can the spice level be adjusted?

Every dish can be made mild, medium or hot. Just tell your server.

Do you offer takeout and delivery?

Yes. Call us for takeout, or order through the major delivery apps across Toronto.
